# AI Paper Analysis Comparator

Use explicitly or when selected for the requested paper workflow. This Skill
is self-contained: acquire missing originals through the shared source role and
prepare missing reports through the shared whole-paper writer and bundled CLI.
Do not install or invoke Finder or Interpreter as a prerequisite.

Read [references/roles/intake.md](references/roles/intake.md) for focused
questions, two-stage approval, scope changes, and per-run subagent authorization.
Follow [references/roles/orchestration.md](references/roles/orchestration.md)
for bounded assignments and minimal contexts. The coordinator owns questions
and scope; workers load only their assigned references. Execute after approval
and Plan Mode exit.

Maintain one visible checklist under
[references/contracts/execution-plan.md](references/contracts/execution-plan.md),
using its text-table fallback when the native plan tool is unavailable.

## Prepare eligible inputs and compare

Inspect the actual corpus, taxonomy, and classification first. Confirm selected
categories, language when ambiguous, missing-material/report work, any taxonomy
or subcategory changes, the complete assignment preview, and one category-wide
concrete everyday scenario. Read
[references/roles/classification.md](references/roles/classification.md) when
preparing or validating assignments. Do not silently repair excluded inputs.

Use only verified and confirmed rows with existing PDFs and complete reports,
passing audits, current content review, and consistent semantic identifiers.
Review a legacy report without current review state only when needed by this run.
A partial paper is ineligible. Missing prerequisite reports follow the shared
[references/roles/report-lifecycle.md](references/roles/report-lifecycle.md)
within the approved scope before category analysis proceeds.

Assign [references/roles/category-writer.md](references/roles/category-writer.md)
to one complete three-section report per category, including singleton
categories. Independent relationship evidence can be prepared in parallel
after authorization; keep the category synthesis, scenario, reading order,
and complete first draft with one writer. The shared lifecycle applies exact
patches first and assigns only residual issues to one fresh reviser. Each relationship requires
the fixed enum, correct direction, and original-source evidence.

Follow the linked lifecycle for checks, review, revision and publication.
Confirm the complete assignment preview before classification/taxonomy/category
publication. Report excluded IDs and reasons in the completion summary.
